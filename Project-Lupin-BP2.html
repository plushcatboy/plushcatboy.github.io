Project Lupin: Blog Post 2


Last week for Project Lupin I created a medically accurate medical wheelchair, which wheels a debilitaed Adam around.

After a week of sickness and inactivity, I found myself with a bored weekend in which to de-rust my Autodesk Maya skills. So I decided to surprise my team and make this. Although I will be using both Maya and Blender for this project, and I model my indie game projects with Blender, I actually think I prefer the inputs Maya has for rapid, high-quality modeling. It felt great to make this medical wheelchair!
